# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Riders booking high-end transportation, Drivers receiving and managing their trips, and Admin (staff) managing dispatch, pricing, and system operations.

## Product Purpose
A reliable, high-end booking and dispatch system for luxury transportation. It must inspire confidence and provide a seamless, premium experience from booking to completion.

## Brand Personality
Exclusive, premium, professional.

## Anti-references
- No cheap or budget ride-sharing vibes (e.g., standard Uber/Lyft patterns).
- No playful, cartoonish, or overly casual UI elements.
- No cluttered, overwhelming enterprise dashboards.

## Design Principles
- **Expert Confidence:** The UI should feel precise, robust, and flawlessly engineered, reflecting the reliability of a luxury service.
- **Understated Elegance:** Use white space and refined typography to let the content breathe, avoiding loud or chaotic layouts.
- **Frictionless Flow:** Both the rider booking experience and the admin dispatch tools must be intuitive and free of unnecessary cognitive load.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA compliance, clear contrast, readable typography.
